Sample pages

Every calendar is made for one community: your name, your dates, and if you like, your own neighborhood pictures.

What every home gets

  • A wall calendar with watercolor art of Northern Virginia through the seasons
  • Your HOA's name and logo on the cover
  • Board meetings, pool opening, yard sale and trash days on each month
  • US holidays plus the celebrations our neighbors observe: Lunar New Year, Ramadan and Eid, Nowruz, Holi, Passover, Diwali, Hanukkah and Christmas
  • A back-page quick reference for the management office, trash day, pool and non-emergency numbers

Optional customizations: make it memorable

  • Your own neighborhood photos (the lake, the clubhouse, the town green), painted in watercolor
  • Local sponsors on each month and the cover, to help with the cost. Sponsors can also order calendars of their own for their clients.
  • A resident photo contest, with each month crediting the neighbor who took the picture
  • Extra pages for community news or a welcome note from the board

Pricing

We're a small local studio, so every calendar is designed for your community, not pulled from a template. Your price has two parts:

  • One-time design, from $199. Your community's name, logo and dates on Mira's watercolor seasons, a proof to review, and your changes.
  • Calendars, priced by quantity. As low as $8 each for 100 or more. Smaller orders are welcome and cost a little more per calendar.

Optional customizations are added to your quote, and local sponsors can cover some or all of it. Tell us your number of homes and we'll send a written quote with design, printing and delivery spelled out. We take orders through December 15; the earlier you order, the sooner calendars reach your homes.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your community with the short form below. No commitment.
  2. A 15-minute call, or email if you prefer. We confirm your pictures, dates, sponsors and quantity.
  3. Written quote and timeline, based on what you chose on the call.
  4. Proof and changes. You review the pages and tell us what to adjust.
  5. Print and delivery once you approve. Payment terms are in your quote.

We are Mira Design Studio

Mira Design Studio is a small Northern Virginia business that creates calendars, posters, flyers, menus and social media graphics for local businesses and communities.

Founded by Shilpa Shri in Ashburn, the studio brings together artwork, local printing and neighborhood partners so every community gets a calendar that feels like home. We live here too, and we know how much a shared calendar on the fridge brings neighbors together.
