Sample pages

Every calendar is made for one community: your name, your dates, and if you like, your own neighborhood pictures.

What every home gets

  • A wall calendar with watercolor art of Northern Virginia through the seasons
  • Your HOA's name and logo on the cover
  • Board meetings, pool opening, yard sale and trash days on each month
  • US holidays plus the celebrations our neighbors observe: Lunar New Year, Ramadan and Eid, Nowruz, Holi, Passover, Diwali, Hanukkah and Christmas
  • A back-page quick reference for the management office, trash day, pool and non-emergency numbers

Optional: sponsors can cover the cost

Each month has a "brought to you by" spot, plus a cover sponsor. Local businesses that already advertise in your newsletter (realtors, dentists, restaurants, tutors) usually cover the print run.

The HOA chooses and collects from its sponsors; we place their logos and handle design, printing and local delivery.

Pricing

Calendars start at $8 each, plus a one-time $199 customization. Price per calendar depends on quantity and how much you personalize. With local sponsors, many communities pay little or nothing.

Email us your number of homes and we'll send an exact quote within one business day.

Printed locally in Loudoun County and delivered to your office. Orders placed by November 20 arrive before the holidays.

How to order

  1. Email us an approximate quantity and your logo (any format).
  2. Send your community dates and any sponsor logos.
  3. We send a proof within 3 business days. Two rounds of changes are included.
  4. Approve the proof and pay a 50% deposit; the rest is due on delivery.
  5. Calendars are printed locally and delivered to your office.

Make it yours

Your dates

Board meetings, pool season, yard sales, trash and recycling days, community events: printed right on each month.

Your pictures

Use our watercolor seasons, or send photos of your own neighborhood (the lake, the clubhouse, the town green). We turn them into matching watercolor art.

Your neighbors

Run a photo contest: residents send their favorite spots, and each month credits the neighbor who took the picture.

We are Mira Design Studio

Mira Design Studio is a small Northern Virginia business that creates calendars, posters, flyers, menus and social media graphics for local businesses and communities.

Founded by Shilpa Shri in Ashburn, the studio brings together artwork, local printing and neighborhood partners so every community gets a calendar that feels like home. We live here too, and we know how much a shared calendar on the fridge brings neighbors together.
