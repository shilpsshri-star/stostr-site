Growth case study

From posts to orders

Radha's Kitchen sells to local families in Katy. The goal is more online orders. Each week an AI agent plans and drafts the social calendar, Radha approves it, it's scheduled on Instagram and Facebook, and these numbers check whether it turned into orders. Tracking started 6 October 2026.

PlanAI→DraftAI→ApproveYOU→ScheduleAI→MeasureYOU→LearnAIYOU

AI: an agent plans the calendar, writes posts, schedules them and proposes the next experiment. YOU: a human checkpoint. Measuring is by hand from Meta Business Suite for now; next it moves to a daily automated pull.
