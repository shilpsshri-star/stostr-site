My story

Why stostr exists

For more than 15 years I built digital products for fintech and corporate payments companies: the systems that make sure orders go through, money lands in the right place and people can see what's happening. Most recently I led AI product and go-to-market work at Framez AI.

Along the way I kept meeting small business owners who were brilliant at what they make and buried in everything around it. A home cook with customers asking the same five questions every day. A designer with beautiful products and an Etsy shop nobody could find. They didn't need another app. They needed someone to set things up properly, once.

That's what stostr does. I set up the boring parts (listings, payments, ordering, trackers) so you can get back to the work you love, and I stay close enough to make sure it keeps working.
